Welcome Home

A church for all people, right here in Las Vegas.

We are a church for all people, with a heart to see lives changed by Jesus as we move forward together, right here in Las Vegas, NV. Whatever brought you here today, we're glad you came — there's a place for you at GateWay.

Campus Pastors

Pastor Danny & Margaret Hand

Pastor Danny and Margaret Hand have been pastoring in Las Vegas for 23 years. They strongly believe that Christians are to be the “best of the best” — free from addictions and all forms of bondage. Danny is a coach at heart who wants to pull God's potential out of everybody who wants to be discipled. He is building and training a team of radical Christians who love God, His people, and have a heart for souls.

"Our mission is simply to Raise Up Champions for Christ."

Part of something bigger. GateWay Vegas is one of seven GateWay City Church campuses across California, Arizona, and Nevada. The wider church family is led by Lead Pastors David and Kathy Cannistraci, who have guided GateWay's growth and care for over 30 years, since its earliest days in 1966 as a 60-member gathering in Sunnyvale, California called Calvary Gospel Temple. GateWay City Church is also part of Ministers Fellowship International, a worldwide family of pastors and leaders.

Wednesdays · 7:00 PM

Breakout Life Group

Are you looking for a place to belong? Our Wednesday night Life Group is the perfect place to find connection, share struggles, and do life together. Join us as we grow together in our faith, find freedom, and discover our purpose.

Every 2nd & 4th Sunday · 10:45 AM – 11:45 AM

Teen Break Out

Calling all Jr. High & High Schoolers! Join us for a fun and engaging experience every 2nd and 4th Sunday, 10:45 AM – 11:45 AM.

Every Sunday · Morning Services

Gateway Kids

Gateway Kids is for kids up through 5th grade. Every Sunday during morning services, your kids get to learn about the Bible in a fun, safe, and age-appropriate environment.

Serve

Become part of Team GateWay

Team GateWay is made up of hundreds of volunteers who call GateWay home. This is the team that makes church happen on Sundays and all throughout the week, and there's a place on it for you.

Prayer Team

This team of trained intercessors covers the weekend services in prayer and prays for people at the close of each service.

Safety Team

We take the safety of all our members, adults and children, seriously. This team helps create a secure environment and stands ready to assist on Sunday mornings.

Ushers Team

Our ushers help manage the large number of attendees in our services, assisting with seating, serving communion, and supporting our prayer teams.

Welcome Team

Guest parking, greeters, and welcome center hosts. First impressions matter, and this team helps every new guest feel right at home.
